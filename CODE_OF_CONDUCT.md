# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this repository to be a respectful place to discuss research code, the
manuscript, and reproducible finance. We pledge to make participation harassment-free
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be civil and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Critique code and claims, not people.
- Respect privacy: do not publish other people's data, credentials, or correspondence.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and report reproducible evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory comments, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block
contributors who violate this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to **mrpandanarang@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially. Maintainers will respond as promptly as possible and may take any action
they deem appropriate, including a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
